Release notes

What changed in each version of Clearcut. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.4 21 November 2026Latest

  • Magic and Colour, made for real fingers. Magic removes the whole patch of one colour under its target, and only when you lift your finger. Slide the target anywhere first; one lift is one Undo, and a second finger calls it off.
  • Colour has the same offset target as Erase and Magic, so your finger no longer hides the spot.
  • The progress message stays up in the Product photo and ID-style photo jobs until the cut-out is ready.

Version 1.3 15 November 2026

  • Photos open again, and a truer Magic. Photos open. In version 1.1 every photo was turned away with “That file couldn't be opened as a picture”, whichever way it was chosen. If you have 1.1, install this version over it.
  • In the Product photo and ID-style photo jobs, the progress message now stays up until the cut-out is finished.
  • Magic removes the whole patch of whatever colour its target passes over, and stops at the subject's edge. Before, it only worked inside a brush circle. Touching down shows the target; the patch goes when the target moves, or when you lift after a tap.
  • Colour has a target that sits clear of your finger, as Erase and Magic do. Aim it, lift your finger, and that patch goes. Tolerance still adjusts the last patch afterwards.
  • Magic and Colour show cross-hairs, so the exact spot is easy to see.

Version 1.1 15 November 2026

  • Other apps, and a colour of its own. An Other apps tab, with a link to download each of the developer's other free apps and a link to its website.
  • Report a problem, in Settings, opens the place to tell the developer about a fault.
  • A new indigo icon, so Clearcut is easy to tell apart on your home screen.
  • The note in Settings on what the app sends, and to whom, is fuller and plainer.

Version 1.0

  • Automatic cut-out with two engines that run on the phone: Fine, built into the app, and Quick, Google's on-phone model.
  • Tap to choose, which shows each thing found in a photo so you can pick the ones to keep.
  • Hand tools: Erase, Magic, Colour, Repair, Hair, Lasso and Shape, with a soft brush, a cursor that sits clear of your finger, pinch to zoom, undo and redo, and hold to compare with the original.
  • Edge finishing: Smooth, Feather, Shift and Fringe.
  • The Studio: a colour, gradient, blurred original, your own photo or a built-in backdrop behind the cut-out, with shadow, outline and colour adjustment, and several cut-outs on one canvas.
  • WhatsApp sticker packs, made in the app and added to WhatsApp with one tap.
  • Quick jobs: Product photo, ID-style photo, Signature or logo, and Batch for up to 50 photos.
  • Saving as PNG, WebP or JPG at a choice of sizes, and sharing to other apps.
  • Photos from your phone's picker, an album list, the camera, or sent from another app with Share.
  • Every finished cut-out kept under Cut-outs, to reopen and carry on with later.
  • A notice on the home screen when a newer version is out.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.4 is called Clearcut.apk. It is 113.6 MB (113,576,135 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

0d71e59eb71227cf577698d16b8d615640bf2ad43c29e988dea47c5f77d7cea6

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Clearcut.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
