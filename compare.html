Free in Clearcut, with adverts or a price elsewhere

Clearcut has no paid version, no subscription, no credits and no adverts. Other well-known background removers pay their way with adverts, with upgrades sold inside the app, or by charging for each picture.

Services that charge by the picture

remove.bg works differently: you buy credits, or a subscription that includes them. Its own help pages say that removing the background from one image costs one credit if you want it in high resolution, and that in its apps even a small preview, of up to a quarter of a megapixel, costs a quarter of a credit.

Clearcut has no credits. Every cut-out is saved at the size you worked on it, as many times as you like, for nothing.

Checked on 15 November 2026 against remove.bg's help page What are image credits?

Where the work is done

Clearcut makes the cut-out on your phone, with or without an internet connection, and your photo is never sent anywhere. The makers' pages checked above do not all say where their apps do the work, so no comparison is made here. If it matters to you, check each app's privacy policy before you give it a photo.

What Clearcut does not do

Clearcut is a background remover, not a design studio. It has no templates, no ready-made scenes, no text tool and no video. If you need those, one of the larger apps will suit you better.
