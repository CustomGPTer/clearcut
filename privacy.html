Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026. This policy covers the Clearcut app for Android (package uk.jj.clearcut) and this website. Clearcut is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Your photos are cut out on your phone. They are never uploaded, to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Clearcut has no accounts and no servers of its own. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • There is no advertising in the app.
  • One part of the app, Google's ML Kit, sends Google technical statistics about how it performs. It does not send your photos. The detail is below.

What stays on your phone

When you open a photo, Clearcut works on a copy held in the app's private storage. Each cut-out you finish is kept there too, with the photo it came from, so that you can reopen it later. Your sticker packs and your settings are kept in the same place.

Pictures you save go to the Pictures/Clearcut folder on your phone, where your gallery and other apps can see them. A picture leaves your phone only when you choose to send it somewhere yourself, with Share or by adding a sticker pack to WhatsApp.

ML Kit is the Google software that runs the Quick engine. Google says that ML Kit processes pictures on the device and does not send them to its servers, and that it collects the statistics above for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google handles them under the Google Privacy Policy; its own account is on the ML Kit data disclosure page. The developer of Clearcut does not receive them.

GitHub and Google receive your device's internet address, as any online service does. The Fine engine is packed inside the app and contacts no one.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Cut-outs kept in the app stay until you delete them: tap one under Cut-outs and choose Delete. Deleting a sticker pack removes its stickers from Clearcut.
  • Pictures in Pictures/Clearcut stay until you delete them from your gallery.
  • Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Clearcut deletes everything it holds. Pictures you saved to Pictures/Clearcut stay on your phone.
  • A sticker pack you added to WhatsApp is WhatsApp's copy. Remove it in WhatsApp.

Your rights

UK and EU data protection law gives you rights over personal data that an organisation holds about you, including the right to see it, correct it and have it deleted.

The developer of Clearcut holds no personal data about you: there is no account, no server and no log of who uses the app. Your pictures are on your own phone, where you control them directly using the steps above. For the statistics Google collects through ML Kit, your rights are exercised with Google. If you need help, use the contact link below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

The app's data is held in private storage that other apps cannot read, and is left out of Android's backups. WhatsApp can read a sticker pack only through a door that Android opens to WhatsApp alone, and only the sticker files themselves pass through it.

The app's connections to GitHub and Google use HTTPS.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub, as the host, receives the internet address of visitors and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app file is downloaded from GitHub as well, on the same terms.

Children

Clearcut is a general-purpose photo tool. It is not directed at children and its developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.

Contact

For a question about this policy or about your data, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please do not include personal details or photos in one.
