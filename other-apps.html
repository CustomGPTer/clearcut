Other apps

Clearcut has siblings. Each is an Android app made by the same developer, free, with no adverts and nothing to unlock. Each button below downloads the latest app file straight from that app's own page on GitHub.

  • Shelfmark

    An ebook reader that sorts your library

    Shelfmark files your books under bookshop headings, so a library of thousands is as easy to browse as a shop. It keeps your place across devices and reads aloud.

    Download Shelfmark for Android

    Visit the Shelfmark website for what it does, its release notes and its privacy policy.

  • Grab

    A video downloader without the adverts

    Grab has its own ad-blocking browser. Open a page with a video on it, tap the round Download button, choose a size, and the file is saved to your phone.

    Download Grab for Android

    Visit the Grab website for what it does, its release notes and its privacy policy.

  • PDF+

    A PDF reader, editor and scanner

    Read, annotate, fill and sign, edit, scan, convert, compress and redact. PDF+ brings the tools other PDF apps charge for into one free app.

    Download PDF+ for Android

    Visit the PDF+ website for what it does, its release notes and its privacy policy.

  • Vigil

    A spare phone as a security camera

    Vigil turns a phone you no longer use into a camera for your home, your pets or your site cabin. It spots people, vehicles and sounds, and saves clips to your own Google Drive.

    In developmentFirst release coming

    The download will be on the Vigil website with the first release.

Installing one

These apps install the same way Clearcut does. Chrome may say the file might be harmful. It shows this for app files that do not come from Google Play. Choose Download anyway. Open the downloaded file, allow your browser to install apps if Android asks, and if Google Play Protect steps in, choose More details, then Install anyway.

Each app has its own website with its release notes, privacy policy and terms, and its own place to report a problem.
